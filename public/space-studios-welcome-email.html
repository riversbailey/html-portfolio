Welcome to Space Studios!

New music coming from Ben Senterfit and Paul Rivers Bailey.

BEN SENTERFIT & PAUL RIVERS BAILEY

Hey friends! It's Ben Senterfit and Paul Rivers Bailey from Space Studios. If you're receiving this message, it's because you've expressed interest in keeping up with us and our creative projects over the years.

We've been busy creating, but releasing to the normal distribution platforms alone has felt like a one-way conversation and left us searching for a better way to share new offerings with you. We're excited to have a new platform to share the music we are making directly with you  to foster a conversation, build a responsive online community, and keep you up to date on what we're doing creatively without all the red tape of streaming services. Our music will still have a home on those services, but through this platform you will get it first and directly.

We've been developing Space Studios for several years, and we finally have a platform to share updates. Newsletters will bring you the music we're making, the projects we're releasing, and other creative work in progress. For now, here are a few releases already out in the world, and some exciting new music coming this fall.

03 — THE EL SUECO PROJECT

El Sueco

Having spent the 1990s in the funk and acid jazz ensemble United Dope Front, Ben has a long history of blurring the line between acoustic and electronic music. These recordings occupy a different part of his brain, and the El Sueco Project is where they live. The moniker came from friend and musical compatriot Javier Gonzalez — you can find Javier's music here.
